Arcade 3D

Five 3D games that run right in your browser. No install, no account. Use the keyboard and mouse; each game shows its controls before you start.

OPEN WORLD

Explore the city

Walk, drive and take boats around a living city with traffic, people and missions on the map.

Play SHOOTER · 4 V 4

Team Deathmatch

Blue squad against red, first to 25. Practise offline against bots.

Play SURVIVAL

Battle Royale

Parachute onto the island, loot weapons and outlast the shrinking safe zone. Play offline against bots.

Play SANDBOX

Destruction Sandbox

Fly, drive and fire rockets to level the whole city, solo with a bot crew.

Play SIMULATION

Rocket Mission

Tour the factory, stack a rocket on the pad and fly it to orbit with live physics telemetry.

Play

This page runs the games on their own, so they play offline and against bots. Live multiplayer, voice chat, the in-game shop and billboards need the full app with its server: see github.com/webgl-games/arcade-3d to run it yourself. Part of webgl-games.
